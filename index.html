Patient Health

Asthma adaptation projects help patients better manage their condition through personalized care plans, regular monitoring, and access to medications. This leads to fewer asthma attacks and complications, allowing individuals to breathe easier and live healthier lives with significantly reduced risk of emergencies or severe respiratory distress.

Awareness

Through community outreach, workshops, and informational resources, patients and caregivers become more informed and proactive. Increased awareness empowers individuals to recognize early warning signs and take timely action, preventing escalation and improving overall disease control and understanding. It will save lifes from danger.

Accuracy

Advanced sensors and AI algorithms provide precise, real-time monitoring of respiratory patterns. This data enables early detection of potential asthma attacks and supports personalized treatment adjustments. High accuracy ensures timely interventions, reducing risks and significantly enhancing patient safety and long-term health outcomes.

Enhanced Life

With better control of asthma symptoms, patients can participate more fully in daily activities, work, and school. They experience fewer disruptions from illness and gain confidence in managing their health. Emotional well-being also improves, as reduced anxiety over asthma attacks fosters a more stable, empowered lifestyle.

Asthma? We care!!

We are a dedicated team of young innovators behind AsthmaX — a smart solution designed to transform asthma care through technology. Our mission is to protect and empower asthma patients by using advanced sensors and artificial intelligence to monitor health, predict attacks, and deliver timely, automatic interventions without human involvement.

With a shared passion for health, innovation, and impact, we strive to reduce emergency cases, improve medication efficiency, and provide patients with a safer, more comfortable life. AsthmaX reflects our commitment to building a healthier future in line with Egypt Vision 2030 and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Product

AsthmaX is a cutting-edge solution designed to revolutionize asthma care through smart monitoring, early intervention, and total support. Using AI-powered analysis, the system continuously tracks vital signs such as heart rate, oxygen levels, and lung sound frequency through wearable sensors. This enables it to detect the earliest signs of an asthma attack—even before the patient feels it. When danger is detected, especially during sleep, a connected micro-device automatically delivers the correct dose of medication through the nose, silently preventing the attack without panic or hospital visits. AsthmaX also provides a full-care ecosystem including personalized alerts, real-time data tracking, 24/7 AI support, and seamless integration with healthcare providers. It’s not just treatment—it’s intelligent protection and peace of mind.
